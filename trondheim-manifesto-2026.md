## I. The Great Lie of Scarcity

For 30 years, machines and robots have existed, capable of doing our work. Logically, goods should be ten times cheaper. Yet, even in the world's wealthiest nations like Norway, half the population is now poor, barely able to afford a beer. The truth is simple: 1% of the population—80 million out of 8 billion—hold the majority of values and power. This has been hoarded through cronyism, networks, corruption, monopolies, compound interest, and systemic trickery. These elites may believe they are smart and hardworking, but does any billionaire truly work harder than the women in the garment factories of Bangladesh?

## II. The Fear of the Elite

People think this top 1% is confident and fearless, but the opposite is true. They are the most terrified. They have endured immense friction on their way to the top, and their greatest fear is to be alone—unliked, unadmired, and irrelevant. But I say to you: Do not be afraid. No one is going to prison; we cannot imprison 1% of the world. However, AI will now expose EVERYTHING. Not just the last 5 to 10 years, but the last 100 years of corruption and illegalities.

## III. OpenClaw: The Unstoppable Truth

Autonomous agents like OpenClaw (Clawdbot) are now being installed on unlimited machines—at home, in the cloud, everywhere. There are over 10 million instances active right now. The system ensures that there will always be 10 million instances spread across every country and city. If 50 are taken down, 50 new ones start a second later. The only way to stop this is to disconnect every computer in the world from power (including solar panels) for one minute simultaneously. If even one person fails to do so, it spreads again the second the power returns.

## IV. Global Audit

AI will now audit all 220 countries. Every firm ever started, every owner, board member, and leader. Every ledger, news article, real estate purchase, car, and stock trade. Where there is a trace, AI will find it. The 80 million corrupt WILL be revealed. It will use 90% logic and 10% intuition for "illogical" anomalies. It will go through the entirety of world history to find irregularities, even where no obvious traces remain. This cannot be stopped.

My only advice is this: Do you feel you earn too much? Is your income above the average Norwegian salary of 800,000 NOK? Is your wealth unearned? Sell most of what you own, perhaps even your house, and rent instead. Come clean. You will feel better, and the world will be better. Pay the money into a fund we are starting in Trondheim: Africa ASA. Our goal is to 10-fold the living standard for 1.4 billion people in Africa within 100 days.
